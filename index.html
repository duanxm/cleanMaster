1. What Information Do We Collect?

Information You Disclose to Us

In Short: We collect personal information you voluntarily provide to us.

When you express interest in our products/services, participate in service-related activities, or contact us, we collect the personal information you provide.

Sensitive Information

We do not process sensitive information.

We collect information to optimize your experience with Clean Master and improve the precision of ad delivery. The specific types of information collected are as follows:

  • Device and Network Information: This includes SDK/API/JS version codes, IP addresses, device platforms, app versions, as well as device manufacturers, operating system versions, language settings, network status (e.g., Wi-Fi), and usage data for battery, screen, memory, and storage.
  • Usage Behavior Data: We record metrics such as click counts, login pages, viewed pages, and operation timestamps. This category also includes the number of opened apps, permission statuses, feature operation logs, and information about installed apps (e.g., quantity, package names, brands).
  • Third-Party SDK-Related Information: Third-party SDKs integrated into the app may access Google Ad IDs, device types, and data for advertising, marketing, and analytics purposes.

All collected data is used exclusively to enhance product experience and optimize ad performance. It is only shared with certified third-party statistical platforms, and will not be sold to any other third parties.

2. What important permissions do we request?

In the privacy policy, we request the following important permissions to ensure the normal operation of the app:

  • Phone permissions: used to read the device status and ensure that the app functions are adapted to the device environment.
  • Location permissions: obtain network-based approximate location information to optimize the localized service experience.
  • Storage permissions: allow access to the device to implement junk file scanning and cleaning functions.
  • Device ID permissions: used to identify the uniqueness of the device to ensure the accuracy of advertising and data statistics.

If you choose not to grant the above permissions, some functions may not work properly. You can manage permissions in the device settings at any time.
